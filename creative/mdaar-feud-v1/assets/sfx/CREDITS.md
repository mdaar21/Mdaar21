# Embedded effects

SoundJay Applause 8 and Laughter & Applause, downloaded 2026-10-30 from
https://www.soundjay.com/applause-sounds-1.html . Terms:
https://www.soundjay.com/tos.html . Terms allow commercial/noncommercial film
and animation incorporation, prohibit standalone redistribution. Original raw
recordings remain in /tmp and are not distributed in this source repository.
Only short incorporated excerpts appear in the finished film mix.

Button sounds, short air transitions and single electronic answer pulses are
original deterministic procedural effects. There is no melody, musical jingle,
music bed or soundtrack.
