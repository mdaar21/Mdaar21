# مدار فيود — commercial v1

Original fictional Saudi studio quiz-show advertisement, 30 seconds, 9:16.
Photoreal still photography with restrained camera animation; this environment
has image generation and HTML motion graphics, not human-performance video
generation. Do not represent this deliverable as filmed actors or full-motion
generated human performances. All five people have blank featureless faces.

## Colors
- #071226 deep navy physical studio and product stage
- #32dcff electric cyan board and podium accents
- #cba561 subtle gold metal and original product identity
- #f8f5eb ivory Arabic typography

## Typography
Original project's Lalezar Arabic display and Noto Sans Arabic for spoken
captions/data, inherited for Arabic coverage and brand consistency. Arabic
letters must never be tracked or split into disconnected character animations.
All scoreboard words are composited as real shaped browser text.

## Motion
Six story sections, 30 fps export at 1080x1920. Photographic dolly drift on
generated stills; brief editorial camera handoffs rather than simulated actor
lip sync. No lip movement. Flip only scoreboard tiles, not Arabic glyphs.
Hold closing product, price, and URL for the final three seconds.

## What NOT to Do
- No music or rhythmic bed. No existing show branding, set, or sound identity.
- No fake survey scores/statistics. Show مشهد تمثيلي / برنامج خيالي.
- No full paid-book pages exposed; only original cover and actual demo UI.
- No invented app interfaces, medical precision, testimonials, or guaranteed results.
- No claims that text-to-speech is a human recording or perceptually verified.
